2. To be · вопросы · отрицания

Сначала определи: у тебя be или обычный глагол?

To be: быть / находиться / чувствовать себя

Время Форма Пример
Present I am · he/she/it is · you/we/they are I am tired.
Past was / were They were late.
Future will be It will be better.
BE + adjective / noun / place
I am a student. She is happy. We are at home.

Как сделать вопрос?

BE + SUBJECT + …?
Are you okay? · Was he late?
DO/DOES/DID + SUBJECT + V1?
Do you like coffee?
Did you go out?
Не смешивай: Are you tired? = состояние; Do you work? = действие.

Отрицание

be + not She isn't at home.
do/does/did + not + V1 He doesn't work.
They didn't go.
modal + not + V1 You shouldn't worry.

3-е лицо: he / she / it

Present Simple: V + S
He works · She likes · It looks
  • -s: work → works
  • -es: watch → watches, go → goes
  • согласная + y → ies: study → studies
После does: Does she work? — -s уже в does.
