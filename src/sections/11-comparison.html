11. Сравнение

Двое → comparative · максимум в группе → superlative · одинаково → as ... as.

Comparative adjectives

short adjective + -er + than
more + long adjective + than
She is taller than her sister.
This room is more comfortable than the kitchen.
big → bigger easy → easier
good → better bad → worse
Сравнение: taller than — двое; the tallest — максимум в группе.

Превосходная степень

короткие прилагательные old → the oldest
big → the biggest
nice → the nicest
окончание -y happy → the happiest
heavy → the heaviest
длинные прилагательные careful → the most careful
interesting → the most interesting
Правило: the -est для коротких слов, the most для длинных; у -y меняем y → iest. Исключения: good → the best, bad → the worst, far → the furthest.

Superlative + опыт

the + superlative + subject + have/has ever + V3
It's the worst film I've ever seen.
What is the most unusual thing you've ever done?
Важно: the обязателен; ever требует Present Perfect — have/has + V3.

Равенство

as + adjective + as
Helen is as tall as Kate.
not as + adjective + as
Mary is not as clever as Julie.
