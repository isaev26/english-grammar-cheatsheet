10. Глагол/прилагательное + предлог

Запоминай устойчивую модель вместе с предлогом и объектом.

Глагол + предлог

МодельПример
be/get on well with + personI get on well with my colleagues.
be friendly with + personShe is friendly with everyone.
look up to + personI look up to my grandmother.
stay in touch with + personWe stay in touch with our friends.
take after + personHe takes after his father.
work with + personI work with my sister.
be part of + groupI'm part of a football team.
have a relationship with + personWe have a good relationship with them.
try + to + V1We try to meet every month.
Местоимение после предлога: with me, after her, не with I и не after she.

Прилагательное + предлог

Чувство или состояние + свой предлог.

be + adjective + preposition + noun / pronoun / V-ing
МодельПример
stressed / worried aboutI'm stressed about the exam.
pleased / happy aboutHe's pleased about his new job.
positive / confident aboutShe's confident about speaking English.
interested inThey're interested in art.
afraid ofHe's afraid of flying.
Частая ошибка: после предлога глагол с -ing: afraid of flying, не afraid of fly, не afraid to flying.
